**Description:**

Rounds is an interval timer built for the treadmill, bike and rower, where you can't keep staring at your phone.

HEAR EVERY CHANGE
Countdown beeps in the last three seconds of every step, a chime when the next one starts, and a voice that tells you what's next: "Level 8, 1 minute". Cues keep playing with the screen locked, on top of your own music.

SEE IT FROM ACROSS THE ROOM
A huge step label, a big countdown and progress rings, with one clear colour per phase: warm-up, work, rest, recovery and cooldown.

BUILD THE WORKOUT YOU ACTUALLY DO
Rounds that differ from each other, blocks with repeats, labels like "Incline 6" or "Speed 12", a different rest on the last repeat, and recovery between rounds. Or just use Quick start: work, rest, repeat.

KEEP TRACK
Every workout is logged with the time you actually exercised. See your week, month or year at a glance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Share templates as files when you want to.
