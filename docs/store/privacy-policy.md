# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Rounds is an interval timer. It does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- **No account.** You don't sign in, and the app doesn't know who you are.
- **No data leaves your device.** Your templates, settings and workout history are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete the app.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.
- **Exports are yours.** When you export templates, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (AirDrop, Files, Mail…) is your choice.
- **Crash reports.** If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports. Rounds adds nothing to them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Mohamad-Sobhie/Rounds/issues.
